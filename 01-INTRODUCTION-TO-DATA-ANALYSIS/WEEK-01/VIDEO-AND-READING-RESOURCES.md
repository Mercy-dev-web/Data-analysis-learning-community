# Week 1 — Recommended Videos and Reading

These resources supplement the written lesson. You are not expected to watch every video on the internet. Follow the required resources first, then use optional resources when you need another explanation.

## Required video

### 1. Alex The Analyst — 2026 Free Data Analyst Bootcamp

[Watch the bootcamp on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnjhHZNJEDk)

**Watch from:** 00:00 to approximately 48:33.

Suggested sections:
- 00:00 — Introduction.
- 15:00 — Data Fundamentals.

**Why this is included:** It provides a practical introduction to data analytics and begins with data fundamentals before moving into technical tools. The full bootcamp is much longer than this week's requirement; do not attempt to finish the entire video in Week 1.

**While watching, write down:**
- How does Alex describe the work of a data analyst?
- What does he say about data fundamentals?
- Which parts of the analyst's work happen before dashboard creation?
- What is one point you would explain differently in your own words?

## Required reading

### 3. Microsoft Learn — Explore the analytics process that turns data into insights

[Open the Microsoft Learn module](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/data-analytics-microsoft/)

Within the learning path, look for the module about the analytics process. Focus on how a business question becomes useful insight and how analysis supports decisions.

**Reading prompt:** Write down three things an analyst should understand before presenting a result to a decision-maker.

## How to use video resources properly

1. Watch with a notebook open.
2. Pause when a new term appears.
3. Write the explanation in your own words.
4. Recreate demonstrations when the video reaches a practical skill.
5. Do not copy a creator's project and present it as your own independent project.
6. If a link stops working, search the creator's official channel for the exact video title. Notify the maintainers so the repository can be updated.

## Video resource policy for this community

- Link to the original creator or official learning platform.
- Do not download and re-upload someone else's video.
- Do not copy a full transcript or course into this repository without permission.
- Keep the lesson notes self-contained; a video is supplementary.
- Review links periodically and replace outdated resources with equivalent current material.

**Reminder:** the purpose of the video is to deepen understanding. Your assignment must still be completed in your own words.
