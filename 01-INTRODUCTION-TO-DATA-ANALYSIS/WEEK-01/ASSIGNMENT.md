# Week 1 Assignment — Write an Analytical Problem Brief

<badge>Assessed deliverable</badge>

## Objective

Demonstrate that you can understand a business problem and plan an analysis before using an analytical tool.

## Choose one scenario

Choose one of the following, or propose a different realistic organisation with enough context to explain the problem.

### Option A — Campus mini-mart
A mini-mart near a university wants to understand why monthly revenue has changed and which products should receive more attention.

### Option B — Online food vendor
A food vendor promotes products on Instagram and WhatsApp but does not know which channel contributes to paid orders.

### Option C — Student support
A department wants to understand patterns in students' course performance and identify where additional academic support may be useful.

### Option D — Delivery business
A small delivery company wants to understand late deliveries and improve its service.

## Your task

Prepare a problem brief of approximately 1–2 pages. Use the headings below.

1. **Organisation and background:** describe the organisation and relevant context.
2. **Problem or opportunity:** explain what needs to be understood. Do not invent a cause.
3. **Stakeholder:** identify who will use the analysis.
4. **Objective:** explain what the analysis should help the stakeholder achieve.
5. **Main analytical question:** write one clear question.
6. **Supporting questions:** write at least five answerable questions.
7. **Metrics:** identify at least four metrics and explain what each measures.
8. **Data required:** list the fields or records needed to answer your questions.
9. **Scope:** define the period, location, products, customers or other boundaries.
10. **Possible limitations:** identify at least three issues that could affect the analysis.
11. **Potential decision:** explain what kind of decision the findings may support. Do not pretend you already know the result.

## Rules

- Do not use Excel, SQL, Power BI or Python for this assignment. This task assesses your thinking, not your software skills.
- Do not invent findings or claim that a cause has been proven.
- Make questions specific enough that data could answer them.
- If you make an assumption, label it clearly.
- Use your own words.

## Submission format

Save as:

`week-01-problem-brief-yourname.md`

You may submit a PDF or document instead, provided all required sections are present and readable.

## Assessment

The assignment is marked using the programme rubric, with special attention to:
- clarity of problem and objective,
- quality of analytical questions,
- appropriate metrics and data requirements,
- realistic limitations,
- logical connection between findings and potential decisions.

**Pass target:** 70%.
