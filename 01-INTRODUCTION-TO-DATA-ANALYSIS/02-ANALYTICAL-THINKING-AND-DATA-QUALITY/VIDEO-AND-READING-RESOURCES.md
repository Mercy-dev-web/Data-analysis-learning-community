# Week 2 — Recommended Videos and Reading

These materials supplement the written lesson. Watch the required video first, then use the additional resources to deepen your understanding.

## Required video 1 — Alex The Analyst

### What is Data Cleaning? | Data Fundamentals for Beginners

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WpX2F2BS3Qc)

**Why it is included:** It introduces data cleaning as a core part of making data usable.

While watching, note:
- What does data cleaning involve?
- Why is cleaning necessary before analysis?
- What can go wrong if an analyst skips this stage?
- Which cleaning actions require context rather than automatic deletion?

## Required video 2 — Alex The Analyst

### Cleaning Data in Excel | Excel Tutorials for Beginners

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jmiEGZ6PIY)

**Why it is included:** It demonstrates practical ways to inspect and clean spreadsheet data. You do not need to master Excel yet; focus on recognising the issues and understanding why each step is taken.

Alex provides a related practice file in the video description. You may use it as optional extra practice, but the community's mini-mart CSV remains the required assignment dataset.

## Required reading 1 — UK Government Data Quality Framework

[Read: The Government Data Quality Framework — Guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-government-data-quality-framework/the-government-data-quality-framework-guidance)

Focus on:
- completeness,
- uniqueness,
- consistency,
- timeliness,
- validity,
- accuracy.

As you read, write one example of each dimension using a Nigerian business or university context.

## Optional reading 2 — Statistics Canada: data quality guidance

[Read: Guidance on Data Quality](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/information-management/guidance-data-quality.html)

Use this for a broader understanding of how data quality depends on relevance, reliability and the intended use of information.

## How to study the resources

1. Read the Week 2 lesson first so you know what to listen for.
2. Watch the required videos and take notes.
3. Pause when you see a cleaning operation you do not understand.
4. Try to identify the same issue in the mini-mart dataset.
5. Write down any action you would not take without more evidence.
6. Complete the exercises before checking the answer guide.
