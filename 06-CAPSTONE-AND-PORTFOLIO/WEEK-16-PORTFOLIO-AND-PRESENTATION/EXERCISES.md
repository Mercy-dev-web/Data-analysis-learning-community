# Week 16 — Exercises

Complete these exercises before starting the final assignment. Use your Week 15 capstone.

## Exercise 1 — The first-time visitor test

Open your project repository in a private/incognito browser window or ask a classmate to review it.

Answer:
1. Can they identify the business question in 30 seconds?
2. Can they find the dataset description?
3. Can they identify which file contains the SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI work?
4. Can they understand the main findings without opening every file?
5. Are there any broken links, missing files or unclear instructions?

Write down three improvements and make them.

## Exercise 2 — Write a project summary

Write 100–150 words covering:
- The business context.
- The dataset and period.
- The tools used.
- The main analysis performed.
- One evidence-based finding.
- One limitation.

Avoid vague phrases such as “I analysed the data and got insights.” Include at least one correctly labelled metric.

## Exercise 3 — Finding or assumption?

Classify each statement as a finding, recommendation or unsupported assumption.

1. “Net revenue was higher in June than in January in the dataset.”
2. “The June increase happened because the company ran a successful advert.”
3. “Management should check campaign records before attributing the increase to advertising.”
4. “Body Lotion had the highest net revenue among the products in this sample.”
5. “Customers in Lagos are more loyal than customers in Abuja.”

**Your task:** Rewrite every unsupported assumption so it becomes a careful question or a testable recommendation.

## Exercise 4 — Improve a weak project bullet

Weak bullet:
“Used Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI to analyse sales data and create dashboard.”

Rewrite it using:
- Action verb.
- Dataset/business context.
- At least two specific analysis tasks.
- Output or business question.
- No invented impact.

## Exercise 5 — Prepare for questions

Write a 2–4 sentence answer to each:
1. What does one row represent in your dataset?
2. How did you check for missing values and duplicates?
3. Why did you choose your main chart types?
4. How did you calculate gross profit?
5. What is one limitation of your analysis?
6. If management gave you another month, what would you investigate?

## Exercise 6 — Repository quality review

Check your repository:
- [ ] README is at the root.
- [ ] Folder and file names are understandable.
- [ ] All links work.
- [ ] The data dictionary explains important columns.
- [ ] Code/notebook instructions are present.
- [ ] Screenshots are readable.
- [ ] Temporary files have been removed.
- [ ] No credentials or private information are exposed.
- [ ] Dataset source and licence/attribution are stated.
- [ ] Findings match the analysis outputs.

## Exercise 7 — Three-minute rehearsal

Give a three-minute explanation without reading every word from your slides. Record yourself if possible. Listen once and note:
- One part that was clear.
- One place you used unexplained jargon.
- One claim that needs evidence.
- One slide or visual that could be removed.

Then practise again.
