# Week 16 — Portfolio and Presentation

**Programme week:** 16 of 16  
**Recommended study time:** 8–12 hours  
**Pass target:** 70%  
**Prerequisites:** Complete Weeks 1–15 and finish the Week 15 capstone analysis.

## Week purpose

You have analysed data. This final week is about making your work understandable, trustworthy and easy for another person to review. You will turn your Week 15 capstone into a portfolio-ready project, organise your GitHub presence, prepare a short presentation and practise explaining your decisions.

You do not need a personal website to finish this week. A well-organised public GitHub repository with a clear README, working files and useful screenshots is enough to begin.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

1. Explain what a data portfolio is and what it can—and cannot—prove.
2. Organise a GitHub profile and project repository so another person can navigate them.
3. Write a project README that explains the business question, data, methods, findings, recommendations and limitations.
4. Present analysis in a short, logical story rather than listing charts or tools.
5. Describe your individual contribution honestly, including where you used tutorials, templates or AI assistance.
6. Add a project to your CV or LinkedIn without presenting a practice project as paid employment.
7. Review your programme work and identify sensible next learning steps.

## What to submit

Create a folder named `week16-portfolio-submission` in your own learning repository (or use a separate portfolio repository). Include:

- A polished Week 15 project folder with a complete README.
- A profile README or a short profile improvement plan.
- At least three project screenshots or visual previews, with useful captions.
- A 5–7 slide presentation or a 5–7 minute recorded walkthrough.
- A 250–400 word reflection on what you learned, what you contributed and what you would improve.
- A completed checklist and a link to your public project repository (if you are ready to publish it).

Do not upload private customer information, API keys, passwords, confidential employer data or files you do not have permission to share.

## Assessment

The final assignment is marked out of 100. A score of **70/100** is the recommended pass mark. A polished design cannot compensate for incorrect analysis, unsupported claims or missing evidence.

## Completion

When you complete Week 16, you have reached the end of the 16-week learning programme. This does not mean you have mastered every tool or are guaranteed a job. It means you have completed a structured foundation and produced work you can continue improving.
