# Videos and Reading Resources

Use these resources to support the week's work. You do not need to complete every resource. Prioritise the GitHub and portfolio material, then choose the sections most relevant to your project.

## Video lessons

1. **Alex The Analyst — 2026 Free Data Analyst Bootcamp**  
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSNXoAs2FtQ  
   Useful sections include building a portfolio website (18:52:39), building a data analyst resume (19:30:07), interview practice (19:47:39) and using LinkedIn to find a job (26:40:14). The video is long; use the relevant sections rather than watching the full bootcamp again.

2. **Alex The Analyst — How to Build a Free Data Analyst Portfolio Site**  
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ocdwh0KYeUs  
   A walkthrough of presenting project work through a portfolio website. A website is optional for this programme; use the video for ideas about organising and explaining projects.

3. **Alex The Analyst — Data Analyst Portfolio Project: SQL Data Exploration**  
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qfyynHBFOsM  
   Use this to observe how a project is documented and how SQL exploration can become part of a portfolio. Do not copy the project and claim it as your own original work.

## How to use these resources

1. Watch one relevant video section.
2. Apply one idea to your own project.
3. Compare the advice with your actual work and audience.
4. Keep what improves clarity; ignore design extras that do not help someone understand the analysis.
5. Record the resource in your README if it materially informed your work.
