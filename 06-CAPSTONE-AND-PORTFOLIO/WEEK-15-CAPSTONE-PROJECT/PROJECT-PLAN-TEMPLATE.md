# Capstone Project Plan

Copy this into your project folder and complete it before analysis.

## Project objective
What business decision should your analysis support?

## Scope
- Period: January–June 2026.
- Business: fictional Nigerian skincare and personal-care retailer.
- Dataset grain: one row per order line.
- Out of scope: customer demographics, advertising spend, stockouts, delivery, operating expenses and satisfaction.

## Business questions
1. What are net revenue, product cost and gross profit?
2. How does net revenue change month by month?
3. Which products/categories contribute most revenue and gross profit?
4. Which cities contribute most revenue?
5. How do Online and Store channels compare?
6. Which customers have repeat order lines?
7. How do discounts affect revenue and gross profit?
8. What limitations should management know?

## Metric definitions
- Gross line sales = Quantity × Unit_Price_NGN
- Net Revenue = Gross line sales − Discount_NGN
- Product Cost = Quantity × Unit_Cost_NGN
- Gross Profit = Net Revenue − Product Cost
- Gross Margin % = Gross Profit ÷ Net Revenue
