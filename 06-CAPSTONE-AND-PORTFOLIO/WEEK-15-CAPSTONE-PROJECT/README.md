# Week 15: Capstone Project — NaijaGlow Retail Performance

**Recommended time:** 8–12 hours minimum; beginners may spread this capstone across two weeks | **Recommended pass mark:** 70%  
**Prerequisite:** Weeks 1–14. This project combines Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI.

## The challenge
You are a junior analyst supporting NaijaGlow, a fictional Nigerian personal-care retailer. Management wants to understand six months of sales performance and receive practical recommendations for the next quarter.

You will work with three related tables: customers, products and sales. You will inspect and prepare the data, analyse it in multiple tools, build a Power BI report and communicate your findings.

## Important definitions
- Net revenue = quantity × unit price × (1 − discount).
- Product cost = quantity × unit cost.
- Gross profit = net revenue − product cost.
- Gross margin % = gross profit ÷ net revenue.
- Orders = distinct OrderID values, not sales-row count.

Gross profit is not net profit; operating expenses are not provided. The data is fictional and should not be presented as real company performance.
