# Capstone Rubric and Submission Checklist

## Assessment (100 marks)

| Area | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Problem understanding, questions and scope | 10 |
| Data understanding and quality checks | 10 |
| Excel formulas, summaries and charts | 10 |
| SQL queries and interpretation | 10 |
| Python workflow, calculations and visuals | 15 |
| Power BI measures, report and interactivity | 15 |
| Cross-tool validation and consistency | 10 |
| Evidence-based findings and recommendations | 10 |
| Documentation and presentation | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Recommended pass mark: 70%.** Important calculation errors must be corrected before a pass.

## Submission checklist
- [ ] Completed project plan and defined business questions.
- [ ] Explained what one row represents.
- [ ] Excel workbook with formulas, at least three summaries and two charts.
- [ ] Commented SQL script answering the business questions.
- [ ] Python notebook that runs top-to-bottom, with data checks and at least three charts.
- [ ] Power BI report with Net Revenue, Gross Profit and Order Lines cards, trend and category/city/channel visuals, plus at least two slicers.
- [ ] README describing the problem, dataset, tools, key findings and reproduction steps.
- [ ] 400–600 word management summary with three or more evidence-backed findings.
- [ ] Two or three practical recommendations.
- [ ] At least three limitations.
- [ ] Presentation of 5–7 slides or a 5–7 minute recording.
- [ ] Figures reconciled across tools.

## Quality expectations
A strong project does more than display charts. It defines a question, uses correct measures, validates figures, explains why visuals matter, makes evidence-based recommendations and states what cannot be concluded.

## Responsible practice
Submit your own work and be ready to explain formulas, SQL and measures. You may use tutorials or AI assistance, but verify generated code and disclose meaningful assistance. Never upload confidential company or personal data. Do not present this synthetic dataset as real business performance.
