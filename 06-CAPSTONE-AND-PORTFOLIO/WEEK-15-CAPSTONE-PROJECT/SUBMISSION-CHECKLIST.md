# Week 15 Capstone Submission Checklist

## Business understanding
- [ ] I explained the business problem and the questions I investigated.
- [ ] I identified what one sales row represents.
- [ ] I kept the raw files unchanged.

## Data preparation
- [ ] I checked row counts, data types, missing values and duplicates.
- [ ] I checked primary and foreign keys.
- [ ] I joined tables using the correct keys.
- [ ] I used one consistent definition for revenue, cost and gross profit.

## Excel
- [ ] I included an analysis-ready sheet.
- [ ] I used formulas or Power Query to bring in lookup information.
- [ ] I included PivotTables and a useful summary/dashboard.

## SQL
- [ ] I included table setup/import notes.
- [ ] I checked record counts and key uniqueness.
- [ ] I included joins and queries for the business questions.
- [ ] I commented queries so another person can understand them.

## Python
- [ ] My notebook or script runs from top to bottom.
- [ ] I used pandas merges with the correct keys.
- [ ] I created calculated measures and summary tables.
- [ ] I exported a cleaned dataset and summary.
- [ ] I included at least three useful charts.

## Power BI
- [ ] I created one-to-many dimension-to-fact relationships.
- [ ] I created measures for revenue, cost, gross profit, orders, units and margin.
- [ ] I built the two required report pages.
- [ ] My charts have titles, labels, currency units and sensible sorting.

## Communication
- [ ] I wrote a 500–700 word management summary.
- [ ] My findings are supported by figures.
- [ ] My recommendations follow from the analysis.
- [ ] I distinguished revenue from gross profit.
- [ ] I stated limitations and assumptions.
- [ ] My README explains the project and how to reproduce it.

**Final test:** Can a person who has never seen your work understand the problem, follow your method, reproduce your results and explain why your recommendations make sense?
