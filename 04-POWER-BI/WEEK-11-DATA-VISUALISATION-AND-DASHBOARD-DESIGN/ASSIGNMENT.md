# Week 11 Assignment: Retail Performance Report

**Pass target: 70/100**

## Scenario
You are supporting the management team of a Nigerian retail business. The team wants a report that helps it understand sales performance across products, categories, cities and channels.

Use the Week 10 model and measures. Your job is to communicate the findings clearly, not simply place as many charts as possible on a page.

## Required deliverable
Create and save a two-page Power BI report as:

`Week11_Retail_Performance_Report.pbix`

### Page 1 — Management Overview
Include:
- Report title and reporting period.
- Cards: Total Sales, Total Units, Unique Customers.
- Total Sales by Category.
- Total Sales by City.
- Total Sales over time.
- At least two useful slicers, including Category or City and Date.

### Page 2 — Product and Channel Detail
Include:
- Total Sales by Product.
- Total Units by Product.
- Sales by Sales Channel.
- A detailed table with Product, Category, Total Units and Total Sales.
- Two or three concise insight statements.

## Functional requirements
- Use Week 10 DAX measures rather than relying on implicit aggregation for key KPIs.
- Format sales values as NGN/₦.
- Sort rankings intentionally.
- Ensure slicers and visual interactions work.
- Use consistent colours, fonts and spacing.
- Include clear titles and units.
- Avoid 3D charts and unnecessary decorative visuals.
- Do not claim that sales value is profit.

## Written interpretation (250–350 words)
Answer:
1. Which category generated the highest sales value?
2. Which city recorded the highest sales value?
3. How does online sales value compare with in-store sales value?
4. What pattern do you notice over the reporting period?
5. What action would you recommend to the retail manager?
6. What important information is missing before the business can assess profitability?

Support every statement with a value or a clear comparison. If you make a recommendation, explain what evidence led you to it.

## Submission checklist
- [ ] Two-page .pbix report.
- [ ] Screenshot of Page 1.
- [ ] Screenshot of Page 2.
- [ ] 250–350 word interpretation.
- [ ] At least three evidence-based insights.
- [ ] A short note explaining your chart choices.
- [ ] Confirmation that slicers and interactions were tested.
