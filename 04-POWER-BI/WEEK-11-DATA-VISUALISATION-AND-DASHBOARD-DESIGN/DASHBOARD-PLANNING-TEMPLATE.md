# Dashboard Planning Template

Complete this short plan before opening Power BI. It helps you design around business questions instead of adding random visuals.

## 1. Audience and purpose
- **Who will use this report?**
- **What decision do they need to make?**
- **What reporting period does it cover?**

## 2. Business questions

| Business question | Measure/field needed | Planned visual |
|---|---|---|
| What is the total sales value? | [Total Sales] | Card |
| Which category contributes most? | Products[Category], [Total Sales] | Bar chart |
| Which city records the highest sales? | Customers[City], [Total Sales] | Bar chart |
| How do sales change over time? | Date[Date], [Total Sales] | Line chart |
| Which products sell the most units? | Products[Product_Name], [Total Units] | Bar chart |

Add or change questions based on the needs of your audience.

## 4. Page layout
Sketch the page using paper, a drawing tool or simple boxes. Mark where the title, cards, charts and slicers should go.

## 5. Insight notes
For each important finding, record:
- What did you observe?
- What number supports it?
- Why might the audience care?
- What should they investigate or do next?

## 6. Final review
- [ ] Does every visual answer a question?
- [ ] Are measures and units clear?
- [ ] Is the most important information easy to find?
- [ ] Are slicers useful and tested?
- [ ] Is the report readable and accessible?
- [ ] Have I avoided claims that the data cannot support?
