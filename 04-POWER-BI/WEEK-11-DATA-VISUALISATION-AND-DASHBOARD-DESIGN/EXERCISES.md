# Week 11 Practice Exercises

Use your Week 10 retail model. Do not create a new model unless your existing file is damaged.

## Part A — Choose the visual
For each question, choose a visual and explain why:
1. What is the total sales value?
2. Which category has the highest sales value?
3. How does sales value change by date?
4. Which city recorded the highest sales value?
5. What are the exact sales and units for each product?
6. How does online sales value compare with in-store sales value?

## Part B — Build the overview page
Create a report page containing:
- A title and reporting period.
- Cards for Total Sales, Total Units and Unique Customers.
- A bar chart for Total Sales by Category.
- A column or bar chart for Total Sales by City.
- A line chart for Total Sales by Date.
- At least two slicers.

## Part C — Formatting review
1. Give every visual a meaningful title.
2. Format currency as NGN/₦ where possible.
3. Sort category and city comparisons by Total Sales, descending.
4. Apply one consistent report theme.
5. Check that labels are readable.
6. Remove any visual that does not answer a useful question.

## Part D — Interactions
1. Select one category in the category chart. Which visuals change?
2. Select a city in the city chart. Which visuals change?
3. Add a Sales Channel slicer and select Online.
4. Test whether the cards and charts respond.
5. Use Edit interactions to set one visual to None, then restore the interaction.
6. Explain why an interaction might be disabled for a particular visual.

## Part E — Detail page
Create a second page with:
- Total Sales by Product.
- Total Units by Product.
- A table with Product, Category, Total Units and Total Sales.
- Sales by Customer City or Segment.
- A short text box with two evidence-based observations.

## Part F — Write insights
Write three insight statements using Observation → Evidence → Meaning → Next step.

## Reflection
1. Why is a bar chart often better than a pie chart for ranking categories?
2. What makes a report overcrowded?
3. What is the difference between a report and a Power BI Service dashboard?
4. Why should a chart title include the measure and category?
5. Why is a chart not the same as an insight?
6. Why should colour not be the only way to communicate meaning?
