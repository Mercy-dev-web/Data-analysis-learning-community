## Study tips
1. Watch the installation/interface video first.
2. Import this week's CSV yourself.
3. Repeat the transformations in the Power Query video on your own dataset.
4. Explain what each transformation changes; do not just copy clicks.
5. Menu positions can differ between software versions. Focus on the concept.
6. Use Microsoft's official download route, not unofficial installer sites.
