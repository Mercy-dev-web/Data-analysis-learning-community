## Suggested study order
1. Watch Alex's relationships video and build the two relationships.
2. Read the star schema article sections on fact and dimension tables.
3. Create the calculated column and measures from the lesson.
4. Read the Microsoft calculation options page to confirm the difference between columns and measures.
5. Test every measure with a slicer. Understanding how filters change a measure is more important than memorising a long list of DAX functions.
