## Suggested viewing plan
1. Watch Alex's EDA video and follow along in your notebook.
2. Read the lesson sections on descriptive statistics and chart selection.
3. Use the Matplotlib quick start when you need help with labels, titles or layout.
4. Complete the exercises before consulting the answer guide.

**Reminder:** A chart is not an insight by itself. Explain what it shows, support the explanation with evidence and state what the data cannot establish.
