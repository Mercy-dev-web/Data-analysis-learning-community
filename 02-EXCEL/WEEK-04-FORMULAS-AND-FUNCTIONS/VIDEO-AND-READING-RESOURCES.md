## Learning tip

When you see a business question, first ask:
- Am I adding values? Consider SUM.
- Am I counting records? Consider COUNTIF or COUNTIFS.
- Am I averaging values? Consider AVERAGEIF or AVERAGEIFS.
- Am I assigning a label based on a condition? Consider IF.
- Must all conditions be true? Consider AND.
- Is at least one condition enough? Consider OR.

The goal is not to memorise every formula. The goal is to understand the question and select the correct tool.
