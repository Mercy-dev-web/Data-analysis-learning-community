# Week 4 — Excel Formulas and Functions

**Phase:** Excel  
**Estimated study time:** 9–12 hours  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Required output:** A sales analysis workbook with working formulas and documented findings.

## What you will learn

- What formulas and functions are, and how Excel calculates them.
- Arithmetic operators, order of operations and formula syntax.
- Cell references: relative, absolute and mixed.
- SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTA and COUNTBLANK.
- IF, AND and OR.
- COUNTIF, COUNTIFS, SUMIF, SUMIFS, AVERAGEIF and AVERAGEIFS.
- Criteria using text, numbers, comparison operators and cell references.
- Common formula errors and ways to troubleshoot them.
- How to turn business questions into formulas and explain the results.

## Required output

Use the mini-mart sales dataset to calculate revenue, classify transactions, and answer business questions using conditional formulas. Submit the workbook and a short explanation of your findings.

**Important:** Formula syntax and argument order matter. In particular, SUMIF and SUMIFS use different argument orders. Read the lesson and practise each function before attempting the assignment.
