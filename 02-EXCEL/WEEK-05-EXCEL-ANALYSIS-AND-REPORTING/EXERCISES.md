# Week 5 — Excel Analysis and Reporting Exercises

Complete these exercises using the supplied `mini_mart_sales_reporting.csv` dataset. Use Excel, not just written answers.

## Part A — Sorting and filtering

1. Sort the transactions by Sale_Date from oldest to newest.
2. Sort the data by Venue A to Z, then Category A to Z.
3. Display only transactions from Ibadan.
4. Display only records where Payment_Status is Pending.
5. Filter transactions where Quantity_Sold is greater than 10.
6. Display Snacks transactions in Oyo.
7. Filter transactions between 8 November and 14 November 2026.
8. Explain why sorting only the Product column would damage the dataset.

## Part B — Conditional formatting

1. Highlight Revenue_NGN values greater than ₦10,000.
2. Highlight all Pending payment statuses.
3. Apply data bars to Revenue_NGN.
4. Apply a colour scale to Quantity_Sold.
5. Highlight duplicate Transaction_ID values.
6. Explain one situation where conditional formatting could help an analyst detect a data-quality issue.
7. Why should you investigate a highlighted duplicate before deleting it?

## Part C — PivotTables

Create a separate PivotTable for each question. Use Revenue_NGN where a revenue total is required.

1. What is the total revenue by Category?
2. What is the total revenue by Venue?
3. How many transactions were recorded by each Salesperson?
4. What is the average Quantity_Sold by Category?
5. What is the total revenue by Venue and Category?
6. How many transactions were Paid and Pending in each Category?
7. What is the total revenue by Payment_Method?
8. Which product generated the highest revenue?
9. What is the total revenue by Sale_Date?
10. Filter the PivotTable to show only Ibadan transactions.

For each PivotTable, state which fields you placed in Rows, Columns, Values and Filters.

## Part D — Charts

Create the following visuals from suitable summaries:

1. A bar or column chart showing total revenue by Category.
2. A bar chart showing total revenue by Venue.
3. A line chart showing daily revenue.
4. A chart showing the number of Paid and Pending transactions by Category.

For each chart, write one sentence explaining why the chart type is appropriate.

## Part E — Interpretation

Answer in plain language, using your PivotTables and charts:

1. Which category generated the highest revenue?
2. Which venue generated the highest revenue?
3. Which salesperson recorded the most transactions?
4. Which payment method was used most often?
5. Which category had the largest number of Pending transactions?
6. What is one limitation of this dataset?
7. Why can you not confidently identify the most profitable category from revenue alone?
8. Write one recommendation that is supported by the results.

## Part F — Dashboard planning

Sketch a dashboard layout on paper or in a blank worksheet. Include:
- a title and reporting period,
- four KPI cards,
- two or three charts,
- one or two filters or slicers if supported,
- two short findings.

Explain why you selected each KPI and chart.
