## Recommended practice order

1. Read the Week 5 lesson sections on sorting, filtering and conditional formatting.
2. Watch Alex's Conditional Formatting chapter and practise.
3. Read the PivotTable section, then watch the 20:00 chapter.

5. Watch the 35:57 Charts and Graphs chapter.
6. Build the charts and dashboard.
7. Complete the exercises before the assessed assignment.

## Important note about Excel versions

Some PivotTable, slicer and PivotChart options differ between desktop Excel, Excel for Mac and Excel for the web. If a feature is unavailable in your version, use the equivalent PivotTable filter and document the limitation. Do not let a version difference stop you from learning the underlying analysis.
