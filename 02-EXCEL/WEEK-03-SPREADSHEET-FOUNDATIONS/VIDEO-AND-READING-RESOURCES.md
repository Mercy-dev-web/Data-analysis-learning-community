# Week 3 — Video and Reading Resources

These resources supplement the written lesson. Watch the relevant sections and practise each action in your own workbook.

## Required video — Kevin Stratvert: Excel Tutorial for Beginners

[Watch: Excel Tutorial for Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LgXzzu68j7M)

This beginner-friendly walkthrough covers the Excel interface, entering data, formatting, Tables, sorting and filtering.

**Recommended sections:**
- 01:30 — Cells, columns and rows
- 02:23 — Entering data
- 03:41 — Formatting cells
- 04:36 — AutoFit contents
- 05:55 — Moving columns and rows
- 06:32 — Tables
- 10:39 — Sorting and filtering

The video continues into formulas, charts and PivotTables. Those topics are covered in later weeks; you do not need to master them now. <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LgXzzu68j7M>

## Official Microsoft reading

- [Overview of Excel tables](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/overview-of-excel-tables) — understand what an Excel Table is and why it helps manage related records.
- [Filter data in a range or table](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/get-started/filter-data-in-a-range-or-table-in-excel) — learn to filter a table or range.
- [Sort data in a range or table](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/sort-data-in-a-range-or-table-in-excel) — learn to sort text, numbers, dates and multiple columns.

## How to use the videos

1. Read the Week 3 lesson first.
2. Watch the relevant video section.
3. Pause and repeat the action in your own workbook.
4. Practise using the community event registration dataset.
5. Complete the exercises and assignment without copying a finished workbook.
